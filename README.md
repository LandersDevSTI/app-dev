# 🍀 Black Clover (ブラッククローバー)

**Black Clover** is a thrilling Japanese anime series based on the manga written and illustrated by *Yūki Tabata*. The story follows two orphans, Asta and Yuno, who grow up together in a world where magic is everything.

## 🎭 Main Characters

* **Asta**: An energetic orphan born without a single drop of magic. Instead, he wields an incredible *Anti-Magic Sword* grimoire.
* **Yuno**: A calm and collected prodigy gifted with immense magical power and the legendary *Four-Leaf Clover* grimoire.

## ✨ Key Themes

1. **Hard Work vs. Talent**: Asta represents pure determination and physical training, while Yuno represents natural genius.
2. **Overcoming Discrimination**: The story heavily covers the divide between commoners/peasants and royal nobles.
3. **Never Giving Up**: Asta's core philosophy drives everyone around him to push past their limits.

## 💬 Favorite Quote

> "My magic is never giving up!" 
> — *Asta*
